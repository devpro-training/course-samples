## Your first command

Let's confirm everything is working.
Click **▶ Run** below:

```bash exec
echo "The lab is alive! 🚀"
```

You should see the output appear in the terminal immediately.

> **Tip:** You can also type directly in the terminal at any time — it's a real shell.
